Lesson Levels

Beginner Lessons

Designed for swimmers who are new to the water. Students learn floating, kicking, breathing, and basic safety skills.

Intermediate Lessons

Students improve strokes, endurance, and swimming techniques while building confidence.

Advanced Lessons

Advanced swimmers focus on performance, stroke improvement, and competitive skills.

Water Safety First

Every Splashes lesson includes important water safety skills to help swimmers stay safe around pools and water.
